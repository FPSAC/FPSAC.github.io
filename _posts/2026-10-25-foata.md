title: "Death of Dominque Foata"
---

We are sad to announce the death of Dominque Foata on 30 September, 2026 at the age of 91.

Dominque Foata was an invited speaker at the 4th Formal Power Series and Algebraic
Combinatorics in 1992 in Montréal.  Along with Adalbert Kerber and Volker Strehl he founded
the mathematics journal Séminaire Lotharingien de Combinatoire.

He is also one of the contributors of the collective M. Lothaire.
